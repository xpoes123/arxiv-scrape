## The One I'd Post First

**Draw a Tree. Now See How Your Culture Drew It. (2607.07267v1)** — the only top idea whose viral mechanic isn't fragile. The provocative-result posts all depend on a caveat surviving reshares or a paradox reliably firing; a personalized "cultural twin" result gets screenshotted before anyone reads the science, has no villain to dunk on, and is backed by real research (45% more cultural signal than text) on a fully public dataset.

**First step:** For a single concept ("tree"), build the country matcher on a curated Quick Draw subset — strip the ~70% timeout scribbles, embed on stroke-order + shape (not pixel overlap), and confirm an uncannily good "your closest country" match on ~20 test sketches in a notebook before touching any front-end. If that one moment feels magical, greenlight the toy; if not, you've spent a day instead of a week finding the fatal risk.
